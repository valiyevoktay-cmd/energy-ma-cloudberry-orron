# Cloudberry / Orrön Energy – pitch deck outline

Five slides, written as an outline. Numbers come from `Cloudberry_Orron_MA_Model.xlsx`.

---

## 1. The question

**Should Cloudberry buy Orrön in an all-share deal at a 25% premium?**

- Offer: SEK 11.00 per share, EUR 286m equity value, EUR 379m EV including Orrön's net debt.
- Paid in about 284m new Cloudberry shares; Orrön holders would own 46% of the combined company.
- Our answer: not at a premium and not at today's power prices.

Visual: offer summary box on the left, a one-line answer on the right.

---

## 2. What Cloudberry would be buying

- About 800 GWh a year of wind, mainly in Sweden, plus JV stakes and a development pipeline.
- 2025: revenue EUR 29m, EBITDA EUR -10m, net result EUR -26m, net debt EUR 90.5m.
- Achieved price EUR 36/MWh in 2025, operating cost EUR 24/MWh.
- G&A of about EUR 9m is large for a company of this size.

Visual: bar chart of revenue and EBITDA, 2024A–2028E.

---

## 3. Why it could fit

- Doubles Cloudberry's output (789 GWh in 2025) in one step.
- Run-of-river hydro peaks in spring and summer, wind in winter: smoother output through the year.
- Most of Orrön's corporate cost can go. Base case: 60% of G&A plus EUR 1/MWh on O&M,
  EUR 6.4m a year from 2028, EUR 78m in present value after tax and integration costs.

Visual: combined monthly generation profile; synergy build-up table.

---

## 4. Why the numbers don't work today

- DCF of Orrön's existing business: EUR 71m. JVs at book: EUR 36m.
- EUR 197m of the price is left to be justified by the pipeline.
- EPS dilution for Cloudberry: -89% in 2027, -63% in 2028, -59% in 2029.
- Leverage rises to 6.5x net debt / EBITDA in 2027 (5.3x standalone).

Visual: value bridge (`value_bridge.png`) and EPS impact by year.

---

## 5. What would change our view

- Power price: the deal is EPS-neutral in 2028 at about EUR 71/MWh (EUR 79 with no synergies).
- Premium matters less than it looks: a nil-premium merger is still about 50% dilutive.
- A deal could work if Cloudberry can show specific pipeline projects worth around EUR 200m,
  or if it buys selected wind farms instead of the whole company.
- Key risks: power prices, refinancing the RCF that matures in July 2027, the Lundin family's
  stake, integration timing.

Visual: EPS accretion vs power price (`accretion_vs_price.png`).
