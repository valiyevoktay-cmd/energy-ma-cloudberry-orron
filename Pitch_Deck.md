# Cloudberry Acquisition of Orrön Energy: Strategic M&A Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: Executive Summary & Strategic Rationale
**Headline:** Cloudberry's proposed acquisition of Orrön Energy creates a premier, diversified Nordic independent power producer (IPP) with enhanced scale and cash flow visibility.

**Bullet Points:**
* **Transaction Overview:** Cloudberry to acquire 100% of Orrön Energy for an implied Enterprise Value of €165M, funded via a mix of equity (27% pro forma ownership for target shareholders) and debt/cash.
* **Strategic Imperative:** Accelerates Cloudberry's transition from a pure-play developer to a scaled operator, immediately adding 150 GWh+ of high-quality, cash-generative wind and hydro assets in the Nordics.
* **Portfolio Diversification:** Merges Cloudberry's strong hydro asset base (primarily Norway) with Orrön's established wind portfolio (primarily Sweden), effectively hedging against extreme localized price volatility (NOA).
* **Financial Profile:** Transaction is highly accretive to 2026E EPS, driven by €5.0M in post-tax operational synergies and an optimized capital structure.

**Visual/Chart Suggestion:** 
* A simple waterfall chart showing Cloudberry Current Capacity + Orrön Capacity = Pro Forma Capacity.
* Logos of both companies with a plus sign, leading to a "Pro Forma Highlights" box.

## Slide 2: Target Overview - Orrön Energy
**Headline:** Orrön Energy offers a highly cash-generative, unlevered portfolio of operational wind assets situated in premium Nordic price areas.

**Bullet Points:**
* **Asset Footprint:** Primarily onshore wind assets in SE-3 and SE-4 (Southern Sweden), capturing structural power price premiums compared to Northern regions.
* **Cash Flow Profile:** High EBITDA margins (~35%) supported by low marginal costs and favorable long-term Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) that secure downside revenue protection.
* **Operational Excellence:** Lean operating model with proven historical capability in asset life extension and repowering optimizations.
* **Strategic Pivot:** Evolved rapidly from its Lundin Energy heritage, transforming into a pure-play renewables operator with a pristine, debt-free balance sheet.

**Visual/Chart Suggestion:** 
* A map of the Nordics with pin drops showing Orrön's key asset locations (heavily weighted to Southern Sweden).
* A small bar chart showing historical and projected EBITDA growth (2024A-2026E).

## Slide 3: Strategic Fit & Synergies
**Headline:** The combination unlocks highly tangible, de-risked synergies through operational consolidation and grid connection optimization.

**Bullet Points:**
* **Grid Connection Arbitrage (Revenue Synergy):** Co-locating new solar or battery energy storage systems (BESS) at Orrön's existing wind grid connection points avoids multi-year interconnection queues and reduces CapEx.
* **O&M Consolidation (Cost Synergy):** Combining maintenance contracts, turbine spare parts procurement, and asset management platforms drives an estimated €5.0M in run-rate post-tax savings.
* **Capital Cost Optimization:** The enlarged entity benefits from a stronger balance sheet and larger asset base, lowering the overall weighted average cost of capital (WACC) for future pipeline development.
* **Complementary Generation:** Hydro (Cloudberry) acts as a natural baseload stabilizer against the intermittent generation profile of Wind (Orrön), creating a smoother, more predictable revenue curve.

**Visual/Chart Suggestion:**
* Two interlocking puzzle pieces or a Venn diagram showing "Cloudberry (Hydro/Development)" + "Orrön (Wind/Operations)" = "Diversified Scaled IPP".
* A table quantifying the €5.0M synergies (e.g., €3M O&M, €2M Corporate SG&A).

## Slide 4: Valuation & Financial Impact
**Headline:** A disciplined valuation framework demonstrates significant value creation, supported by robust DCF fundamentals and conservative synergy assumptions.

**Bullet Points:**
* **Valuation Methodology:** Implied Enterprise Value derived via a 5-year Unlevered DCF model, utilizing an 8.5% WACC and a 2.0% Terminal Growth Rate.
* **WACC Justification:** 8.5% reflects the current normalized risk-free rate environment in Europe, applying a standard equity risk premium for Nordic IPPs and a conservative beta.
* **EPS Accretion:** The transaction is highly accretive to Cloudberry’s 2026E Pro Forma EPS, primarily driven by Orrön's immediate cash flow generation and the realization of cost synergies.
* **Balance Sheet Health:** Even after raising €4.2M in new debt to fund the cash portion, the pro forma entity maintains conservative leverage ratios, preserving dry powder for future M&A.

**Visual/Chart Suggestion:**
* Football field valuation chart showing DCF range vs. Precedent Transactions vs. Current Implied Offer.
* A simple bar chart showing Cloudberry Standalone 2026E EPS vs. Pro Forma 2026E EPS.

## Slide 5: Key Risks & Mitigants
**Headline:** While the strategic logic is sound, integration and macro-environmental risks must be proactively managed to protect shareholder value.

**Bullet Points:**
* **Risk: Power Price Volatility.** Nordic wholesale prices (Nord Pool) have normalized significantly from 2022 peaks, pressuring merchant revenues.
  * *Mitigant:* Implementing a blended hedging strategy (PPAs and financial hedges) to lock in minimum baseload pricing for 50-60% of expected generation.
* **Risk: Cannibalization Effect.** As more wind is built in the Nordics, prices drop precisely when the wind blows (capture rate degradation).
  * *Mitigant:* The combined portfolio’s heavy hydro weighting acts as a physical battery, allowing generation to be curtailed when wind prices are negative and dispatched during premium hours.
* **Risk: Integration Headwinds.** Delays in realizing the €5.0M O&M cost synergies due to misaligned IT or corporate cultures.
  * *Mitigant:* Straightforward integration given both are Nordic-headquartered entities with similar lean-corporate, asset-heavy operating models.

**Visual/Chart Suggestion:**
* A simple "Red/Amber/Green" risk matrix with "Likelihood" on the X-axis and "Impact" on the Y-axis.
